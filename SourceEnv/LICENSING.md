## Competition license review

The official [Paper Track rules](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/gemma-4-developer-agent-paper/rules), retrieved on 2026-10-14, specify **Open Source Apache 2.0** for winners. Specific section 2.5 covers the winning submission and the source used to generate it; it explicitly excepts unowned generally commercially available software procurable without undue expense, and incompatible-license input data or pretrained models. Section 2.8 calls for code/documentation capable of reproducing the submission, resource/environment information, the required grant and representation of the right to make it. The Apache option is prepared to supply the named license for owned material while preserving MIT. This preparation is not organizer acceptance or submission.

For later publication, foundational section 3.6 requires public Competition Code sharing through the competition's Kaggle discussion forum or notebooks as well. Specific section 2.4 restricts redistribution of Competition Data to nonparticipants. Obtain restricted official inputs through their authorized source; do not bundle them. The Paper Track's Submission Requirements say an attached private Kaggle resource becomes public after the deadline: do not attach the historical private repository or private evidence. Full retained rule sections and selectors are in the review package's `sources/KAGGLE_PRIMARY_VERIFICATION.json`.
